Design notes & next steps
Reception, acknowledgement, repair, and closure are inspired by communication grounding. Responsive care is an exploratory analogy from interpersonal research. Color associations vary across people and cultures. No color, motion, or timing here has been validated as universal.
Reserved extensions: negotiation, shared attention, recalled scenes, and shared waiting. No voice, hardware connection, accounts, or remote analytics in this prototype.
